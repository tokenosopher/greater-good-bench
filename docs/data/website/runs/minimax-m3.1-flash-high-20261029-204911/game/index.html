    03 / COUNT IT HONESTLY

    Directional math.
    Human judgment.

    Common Ground combines conservative public estimates with simple multipliers. It shows scale; it does not replace local research or lived expertise.

    See the assumptions ＋
    • Each action has a per-person estimate for emissions, money, connection time, and material kept in use.
    • Weekly estimates are multiplied by four; monthly estimates are counted once.
    • Emissions use widely cited food, transport, waste, and energy ranges, rounded conservatively.
    • Social impact counts meaningful contact time, not the number of likes or impressions.
    • Regional context changes recommendations, but never turns estimates into guarantees.
