SCROLL TO CONNECT

A small but radical idea

Most conflicts aren’t about
who’s right.

They’re about something that matters, asking to be heard. Four small shifts help us hear it.

  1. 01

    Start with the moment

    Describe what a camera could capture. No motives, labels, or “always” and “never.”

    Observation, not evaluation
  2. 02

    Name your inner weather

    “Hurt” opens a door that “you don’t care” closes. Feelings are information, not indictments.

    Feelings, not accusations
  3. 03

    Find what matters

    Under frustration is often a need for respect, rest, clarity, trust, or connection.

    Needs, not blame
  4. 04

    Make a doable ask

    Invite one specific, positive action. A real request leaves room for the other person to say no.

    Requests, not demands
