        Why this, why now

        If everyone who can read this got a little clearer, a little plainer, and a little kinder, the same disagreements, instructions, and decisions would produce far less confusion and far less harm. That’s the whole idea. Nothing here is magic — it’s the disciplined practice of ordinary goodness.

        ✎ Clear words are a kindness

        Most of the world reads in a language they didn’t grow up with, or with limited literacy, or in a hurry. Every needless long word is a lock. Plain language is how knowledge, rights, and safety actually reach people.

        ◎ Slow decisions age well

        Most regret comes not from failing to decide, but from deciding with only one perspective in the room. Cheap structure — naming who’s affected, and asking whether the rule scales to everyone — beats cleverness.

        ♥ Kindness is a habit, not a mood

        The people who change communities aren’t unusually saintly; they’ve just made small generous acts routine. Habits are built by repeating tiny, scheduled things — exactly what a compounding chart makes visible.

        🪶 Private by design

        No accounts, no trackers, no requests to any server. Your decisions and messages are yours alone — they live only in this tab. Kindness includes not treating people as data.
