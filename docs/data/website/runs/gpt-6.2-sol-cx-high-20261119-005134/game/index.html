How it works

Start with what
someone needs.

  1. Pick something you can do.

    Choose an action that fits your day and helps a real person or group.

  2. Ask what would help.

    Give people a say in what you offer. For donations or volunteering, check what the group needs first.

  3. Decide when you’ll do it.

    Make a specific plan and keep a copy somewhere you’ll see it.
