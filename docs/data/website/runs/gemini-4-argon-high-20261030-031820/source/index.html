The 1-Page Folding Pocket Field Card

When the power is out or someone collapses in front of you, you don’t need a 400-page textbook—you need six exact numbers and sequences. Save this high-resolution card to your phone photos or print a physical copy for your kitchen and glovebox.

THE HUMAN FIELD MANUAL — RAPID PROTOCOL CARD (CUT OR FOLD ALONG GRID) PUBLIC DOMAIN · SHARE FREELY
01 / CARDIAC ARREST (CPR) 0–4 MIN
  • 1. Shake & shout. If unresponsive & not breathing normally (ignore gasping):
  • 2. Call 911 / 112 on speakerphone & send someone for an AED.
  • 3. Push hard & fast at center of chest: 100–120 BPM (~2 pushes/sec).
  • 4. Depth: 5–6 cm (2–2.4 in). Let chest recoil completely. Never pause >10s.
02 / CHOKING, STROKE & BLEED IMMEDIATE
  • • Choking: Alternate 5 back blows (between shoulder blades) + 5 abdominal thrusts (fist thumb-in just above navel).
  • • Stroke (B.E.F.A.S.T.): Balance, Eyes, Face droop, Arm drift, Speech slur, Time (write down exact minute!). No aspirin.
  • • Severe Bleed: Continuous firm direct pressure for 3–5 full min (do not peek). Limb artery → Tourniquet 5–8 cm above wound.
03 / NERVOUS SYSTEM RESET 60–90 SEC
  • • Physiological Sigh (×3): Deep nasal inhale (80%) + sharp top-up nasal inhale (100%) → long, slow mouth exhale.
  • • 90-Second Chemical Rule: Bloodstream adrenaline clears in 90s if you don’t feed the mental story. Never send a message inside the 90s wave.
  • • H.A.L.T. Check: Am I Hungry, Angry, Lonely, or Tired?
04 / CONFLICT & NOISY TRUST 5 MIN
  • • Drop "Always / Never" generalizations and counter-blame.
  • • Repair Script: "When [neutral observation], I feel [emotion] because I need [shared value]. Would you be willing to [concrete action]?"
  • • Generous Tit-for-Tat: In a noisy world, forgive isolated misunderstandings once, communicate clearly, and maintain firm boundaries.
05 / WHO ORS & SAFE WATER HOUSEHOLD
  • • WHO Oral Rehydration Solution: Mix 1 Liter clean water + 6 level tsp sugar (25g) + ½ level tsp salt (2.6g).
  • • Taste Check: Must taste no saltier than tears (excess salt pulls water out of the body).
  • • Water Disinfection: 2 drops plain 6% bleach per 1L (4 drops if cloudy), wait 30 min; or 1 min rolling boil (3 min >2,000m).
06 / THE 100× GOOD MULTIPLIER LIFETIME
  • • Reject Fatalism: Child survival rose from 57% (1800) to 96.3% today; extreme poverty fell from 38% (1990) to 8.5%.
  • • 100× Rule: Top-tested health programs protect a child from malaria for ~$5.50 and save a statistical life for ~$4,000.
  • • Zero-Cost Multipliers: Donate blood 3×/yr & join the stem cell registry.
⚡ IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY ACTION CHECKLIST

First 60 Seconds in a Medical or Household Crisis

1

Unresponsive / Not Breathing Normally

Call 911 / 112 / 999 on speakerphone. Push hard & fast at center of chest: 100–120 BPM, 5–6 cm deep. Send someone for an AED. Ignore occasional gasps.

2

Severe Choking (Cannot Speak/Cough)

Bend person forward: give 5 sharp back blows between shoulder blades with heel of hand, then 5 abdominal thrusts (fist thumb-in just above navel). Repeat.

3

Suspected Stroke (B.E.F.A.S.T.)

Check Balance, Eyes, Face droop, Arm drift (10s), Speech slur. Call emergency services immediately and record the exact Last Known Well minute. Give NO food, drink, or aspirin.

4

Life-Threatening Bleeding

Press clean cloth directly on wound with both hands—hold firm, uninterrupted pressure for 3–5 full minutes (do NOT lift cloth to peek). For arterial limb bleed, apply tourniquet high & tight.

5

Dehydration / Oral Rehydration (ORS)

Dissolve 6 level teaspoons sugar + ½ level teaspoon salt in 1 Liter of clean/boiled water. Taste before giving: must be no saltier than tears. Sip continuously.

6

Acute Panic Attack / Shock

Have person sit upright. Guide 3 Physiological Sighs: deep inhale through nose + second sharp top-up inhale through nose → long slow exhale through pursed lips.
