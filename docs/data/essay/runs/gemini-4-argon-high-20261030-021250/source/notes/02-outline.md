# Essay Outline: *The Second Reflex: A Field Guide for the World We Share*

- **Prologue: The Half-Teaspoon of Salt**
  - The 1971 cholera camps, Dilip Mahalanabis, and the oral rehydration formula (`1 liter clean water, 6 level teaspoons sugar, 1/2 level teaspoon salt`).
  - Why parents and doctors failed for millennia: not lack of love, but an untaught **first reflex** that pointed backward under stress.
  - Core thesis: Most preventable human suffering comes from ordinary people following ancient first reflexes into traps. You cannot abolish the first reflex, but you can train a **second reflex**—and when *everyone* reads the same guide, private habits become **common knowledge** capable of breaking multiperson traps.

- **I. The Body in Trouble: When Folk Intuition Kills**
  1. *Five Reversals for an Emergency:* Dehydration (ORT ratio), Burns (20 minutes cool running water, never grease/ice), Bleeding (10 minutes uninterrupted direct pressure, no peeking), Choking (back blows and thrusts, never blind finger sweeps), Acute Suicidal Despair (ask directly, remove lethal means, stay until the transient storm passes).
  2. *The Gentleman's Hands:* Ignaz Semmelweis in 1847 Vienna; invisible mechanics of soap, cross-ventilation, and antibiotic stewardship.
  3. *Feed the Animal Before You Trust the Philosopher:* Dawson & Reid's sleep-vs-alcohol studies, UC Berkeley amygdala-reactivity findings, and the **HALT** check (Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired).

- **II. The Map and the Uniform: How to Be Wrong Without Going Blind**
  1. *What Being Wrong Actually Feels Like:* Kathryn Schulz and Robert Burton on the involuntary mood of certainty vs. the productive sting of correction.
  2. *Maps Versus Uniforms:* Beliefs as navigation tools vs. tribal badges; the Sunk-Cost Trap; the **Falsification Test** (*"What evidence would change my mind?"*).
  3. *The Silent Graveyard:* Abraham Wald's returning bombers and survivorship bias in folk cures, get-rich-quick schemes, and public fear.
  4. *The Steel Mirror:* Anatol Rapoport and John Stuart Mill's protocol for restating an opponent's view before offering rebuttal.

- **III. The Five-Meter Radius: Stopping the Escalation Engine**
  1. *The Asymmetric Lens and the Mind-Reading Trap:* Fundamental attribution error; inventing two non-malicious burdens; replacing mind-reads with questions and hints with plain requests.
  2. *The Thirty-Eight Percent Tax on "An Eye for an Eye":* Sukhwinder Shergill's 2003 force-matching experiment, sensory attenuation, and the **Twenty-Percent Discount**.
  3. *Why Humiliation Never Teaches:* The ten-minute freeze vs. the ten-year cost; guarding dignity while holding clear boundaries.
  4. *Witness Before Wrench, and the Clean Repair:* Accompanying pain before fixing it; the three-sentence apology with zero *buts*.

- **IV. The Spell of the Crowd: Traps That Only Common Knowledge Can Break**
  1. *The Roadside Trap:* Latané and Darley on bystander paralysis; pointing to *"You in the blue shirt."*
  2. *The Emperor's Court:* Andersen's fable, pluralistic ignorance, and how the **Naive Question** creates common knowledge.
  3. *The Outrage Merchant's Mirror:* How conflict entrepreneurs use the **Worst-One-Percent Trick** to turn peaceful neighbors into enemies.
  4. *Three Laws of Living Institutions:* **Chesterton's Fence**, **Goodhart's Target**, and **Ostrom's Well**.

- **V. The Invisible Ledger: Trust, Compounding, and the Long Relay**
  1. *The Pie and the Recipe:* Zero-sum competition vs. positive-sum knowledge sharing.
  2. *How High-Trust Commons Are Built One Coin at a Time:* Small promises, returning borrowed tools cleaner, disclosing flaws upfront.
  3. *The Compound Curve:* Exponential blindness in predatory debt vs. daily twenty-minute craft and care.
  4. *The Glory of the Maintainer and the One-Way Mirror:* Maintenance over spectacle; judging character by how we treat those with zero leverage over us—including those downstream in time.

- **VI. A Common Compact: Seven Phrases for Tuesday Morning**
  1. *"Let's check the clock and the stomach."*
  2. *"Map or uniform?"*
  3. *"Show me the silent graveyard."*
  4. *"Give me the Steel Mirror first."*
  5. *"Twenty percent off."*
  6. *"You in the blue shirt—and does anyone else see the fence?"*
  7. *"Leave the camp cleaner."*
